Technical Overview

SHALFA brings embedded systems, computer vision, sensing, communication and autonomous control together in one functional unmanned surface vehicle prototype. A Raspberry Pi 4 runs the Python software that combines camera and MLX90640 thermal sensing with GPS awareness and YOLOv5 detection, and drives the boat through manual or autonomous differential motor control with a smart escape behaviour. A web interface presents the live camera feed, thermal visualization and GPS status, while a safe mission simulation layer of LED indicators and audio effects demonstrates operational scenarios without real weapons or hazardous materials.
